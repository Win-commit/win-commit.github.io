I am Runze Li, a full-time Research Assistant now at MiLab at Westlake University, advised by Professor [Donglin Wang](https://milab.westlake.edu.cn/). My research focuses on embodied intelligence and robot foundation models, especially Vision-Language-Action models, world models, and generalist robotic agents for instruction understanding, implicit planning, and action generation.


Previously, I worked remotely with Professor [Jordan Boyd-Graber](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~jbg/) at the University of Maryland on natural-language-guided image geolocation, where I helped develop the NAVIG framework and built data collection and evaluation pipelines for image geolocation with vision-language models. I also conducted research at DisLab at Nanjing University with Professor [Wenzhong Li](https://cs.nju.edu.cn/lwz), working on deep learning methods for video super-resolution.

I received dual Bachelor's degrees in Computer Science and Financial Engineering from Nanjing University.

Goal: Advancing toward general-purpose robotic agents through embodied intelligence.

Focus: Building robust, continual, and generalizable robotic systems with Vision-Language-Action models and world models.

Email: lirunze [AT] westlake.edu.cn
